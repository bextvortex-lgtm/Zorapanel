ZoraPanel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

ZoraPanel is a VTU and digital solutions platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ZoraPanel, we may collect:

  • Username and full name.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Account and authentication information.
  • Wallet balance and transaction records.
  • Service details needed to process your requests.
  • Technical information such as browser and device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts.
  • Provide VTU and digital solutions services.
  • Maintain wallet and transaction records.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Protect accounts and prevent fraud or unauthorized activity.
  • Improve platform performance and security.

3. Email Address and Account Security

Your registered email address identifies your account. Customers cannot change their registered email address through the profile page. Keep your password confidential and notify us if you suspect unauthorized access.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the platform, process transactions, maintain security or comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Data Protection

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, protect the platform and meet applicable legal obligations.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security or legitimate operational reasons.

8. Cookies and Technical Information

Our website and supporting services may use cookies or similar technologies for authentication, security and functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

ZoraPanel is not intended for children who are below the minimum age permitted to use the services under applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact ZoraPanel through the official customer support channel published on our website.
